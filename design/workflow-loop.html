AI

Orient full act

Read every scene in the act, explore the codebase, plan the whole build
  • Read all scenes in the act file — understand the complete arc
  • Read the “Previously” header — what was built in prior acts
  • Explore src/ — trace components, state, IPC, data model
  • Read LESSONS.md — load constraints from prior sessions
  • Map scene dependencies — determine build order
  • Output: per-scene approach summary + build order + risks
2
PM

Align full act

Review the AI’s plan for the entire act — does it match your vision?
  • Read per-scene summaries + build order (~5 min)
  • Yes — AI proceeds to build all scenes autonomously
  • Adjust — correct priorities, reorder, deprioritize scenes
  • Wrong arc — the act’s story needs rethinking
This is your last touch before the AI builds everything. After you approve, the AI works autonomously through all scenes. You come back when the entire act is ready to experience.

PM

Experience full act

Walk the entire act’s user journey — does the arc land?
  • AI provides: per-scene verification report (beats passed, commits, any notes)
  • Run the app and walk the act’s story start to finish
  • You’re judging the arc, not individual features
  • Ship it — the act lands, move to the next act
  • Adjust scenes — “3.1 and 3.2 are great, 3.4 shortcuts feel wrong” — AI fixes specific scenes (bounded inner loop)
  • Rethink — the story itself needs updating, back to the act file
Visual gaps caught here. The a11y walkthrough catches functional and structural issues (~80%). Color, spacing, animation, and feel are caught by you. Those are CSS tweaks, not architecture rewrites.

  • LESSONS.md — every revert or multi-attempt fix = mandatory entry
  • ARCHITECTURE.md — system structure changes from this act

The contract

You describe the experience, one act at a time. The AI plans the full act, you approve once. The AI builds every scene autonomously — verifying each against the act file using the accessibility tree before moving to the next. You come back to walk the complete arc. Same file, same standard. When the arc lands, it ships.
